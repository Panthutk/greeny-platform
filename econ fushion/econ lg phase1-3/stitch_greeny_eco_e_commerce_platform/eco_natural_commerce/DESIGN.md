## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a fresh, vibrant, and approachable vision of sustainable Thai commerce. Moving away from muted, dusty "kraft-paper" tropes, the aesthetic captures the feeling of crisp organic markets, contemporary botanical packaging, and brisk modern digital storefronts.

### Visual Pillars
- **Vitality & Freshness:** High-contrast daylight surfaces paired with lively leaf-greens impart vitality, health, and ethical transparency.
- **Retail Precision:** Dynamic urgency cues (flash-sale ribbons, savings markers, stock meters) adopt crisp, vibrant coral tones to ensure seamless high-volume commercial utility without compromising environmental warmth.
- **Tactile Purity:** Smooth, pillowed containers, generous white space, and subtle organic borders evoke clean modern eco-friendly packaging and polished consumer unboxings.

## Colors

The palette balances clean agricultural freshness with conversion-optimized retail clarity.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#2EB82E` / Fresh Leaf Green):** Core actions, primary buttons, verified eco-credentials, in-stock indicators, and active navigation items.
- **Secondary (`#142918` / Forest Slate):** Deep evergreen neutral serving high-contrast typography, crisp icons, dark structural footers, and active selection state outlines.
- **Tertiary (`#F04A42` / Bright Coral Accent):** Deal urgency, flash sales, countdown badges, discount ribbons, price reductions, and vital system alerts.
- **Neutral (`#717E73` / Herbal Slate Neutral):** Low-emphasis secondary typography, inactive icons, subtle dividers, and empty states.
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: `#FAFAFA`
  - Elevated cards & sheets: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle product wells: `#F2F5F2`
  - Fresh brand tint / badge fill: `#EAF8EB`
  - Flash tint fill: `#FDF1F0`

## Typography

The typography system pairs geometric warmth with high-legibility loopless proportions, echoing modern Thai sans faces (such as Prompt and Kanit). 

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with balanced counters and open apertures that translate directly to clean contemporary retail banners, merchant headlines, and pricing tiers.
- **Body & Captions (Be Vietnam Pro):** Chosen for its generous x-height, neutral tone, and comfortable rhythm across dense item specifications, customer reviews, and multilingual catalog tags.
- **Numbers & Monetary Values:** Utilize `Plus Jakarta Sans` with bold or semi-bold weights (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to anchor price comparisons and countdown matrices cleanly.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive fluid grid anchors all shopping interactions, scaled dynamically from handheld device viewports up to large desktop viewports.

### Grid Framework
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` (16px) margins, `1rem` gutters. Product feeds default to a 2-column card split.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` (32px) margins, `1.25rem` gutters. Product grids expand to 3 or 4 columns.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxed at `1320px` container width with `3rem` canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Standard listing grid accommodates 5 or 6 items per row.

### Spacing Principles
- Compact retail rhythm: Use `space-xs` and `space-sm` for internal component tight-locks (star rating with count, price with currency indicator, badge icons).
- Section flow: Separate commercial rows (Flash Deals, Recommended Stores, Categories) with `space-xl` gaps to maintain airy scannability.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean tonal layers combined with soft, natural-tinted ambient shadows to evoke crisp physical packaging sitting on smooth surfaces.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas):** Pure daylight slate-white `#FAFAFA`.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards):** `#FFFFFF` bordered by a 1px soft vegetal border (`rgba(20, 41, 24, 0.06)`). No sharp black shadows.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Hover & Quick-Shop Overlays):** Ambient shadow tinted with forest-slate: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(20, 41, 24, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(20, 41, 24, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Bars & Bottom Cart Sheets):** Elevated sticky elements: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(20, 41, 24, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs smooth, friendly curvatures that mimic botanical contours and recycled ergonomic packaging.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to form fields, inline chips, discount tags, and mini thumbnails.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, `1rem` / 16px):** Standard for product listing cards, seller shop banners, and modal dialogs.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Reserved for category selection chips, sticky CTA pill buttons, organic certification pills, and live countdown badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest Leaf Green background (`#2EB82E`), white text, bold label. Subtle hover lift with brightness shift to `#279F27`.
- **Secondary / Urgent CTA:** Coral Red background (`#F04A42`), white text, applied strictly for "Buy Now" checkout buttons and Flash Sale claims.
- **Outline / Ghost:** 1.5px border in `#142918`, transparent fill, hover fill at 4% Forest Slate tint.
- **Sizes:** Large (48px height, rounded-full, for bottom checkouts), Medium (40px, default), Small (32px, product card quick-add).

### Cards
- **Product Listing Card:** White background, 16px corner radius, hairline eco-border (`rgba(20, 41, 24, 0.06)`). Product images sit inside an edge-to-edge subtle grey-green well (`#F5F8F5`). Includes floating discount pill (top-left) and wishlist toggle (top-right).
- **Merchant / Eco-Shop Card:** White surface featuring merchant avatar, green leaf verification badge, review star score, and sustainable fulfillment tags (e.g., "Plastic-Free Packaging").

### Badges & Micro-Tags
- **Mall / Verified Badge:** Deep forest slate container (`#142918`) with crisp white micro-type and an emerald leaf icon.
- **Flash Discount Pill:** Vibrant coral container (`#F04A42`) with bold white text (e.g., `-35%`).
- **Eco Certification Tag:** Soft botanical fill (`#EAF8EB`), Forest Slate text (`#142918`), and green check icon (e.g., "100% Organic", "Biodegradable").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped 44px container, `#F2F5F2` fill, no border in default state, transitioning to a crisp 1.5px `#2EB82E` outline on focus. Prefixed by magnifying glass and leaf search prompt.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded 4px checkboxes and circular radios with a 2px `#717E73` stroke, filling with `#2EB82E` and showing a white checkmark when active.

### Chips & Category Tabs
- **Category Pill:** Pill-shaped, `#FFFFFF` surface with a subtle 1px border. Selected state swaps to solid `#142918` with pure white text and active shadow.
- **Filter Chip:** Features an icon prefix and trailing dismissal cross; adopts `#EAF8EB` fill when active.
